# Public source notes — Candi Sambisari

Checked 19 October 2026. This is an independent visitor guide, not an operator website.

- [Jogja Cagar](https://jogjacagar.jogjaprov.go.id/detail/430/candi-sambisari): one main temple and three perwara; discovery by a farmer in 1966; staged reconstruction/restoration and completed restoration in 1987. Scholarly dating interpretations differ across the 8th to 9th–10th centuries. Do not present a precise construction year or royal attribution as certain, or assume a specific eruption caused burial.
- [National cultural registry](https://budaya.data.kemdikbud.go.id/cagarbudaya/objek/KB000070): original construction dating remains uncertain.
- [Visiting Jogja, 2020](https://visitingjogja.jogjaprov.go.id/en/17327/the-underground-temple-of-yogyakarta-sambisari-temple/): provincial visitor context. The site is open-air and roughly 6.5 metres below surrounding ground level; this does not establish tourist tunnels or entry to every room.

Candi Sambisari, Purwomartani, Kalasan, Sleman 55571. Third-party dated listing, not a guarantee of holidays, last admission, night access or all areas.
